# Hayatan 7.1.6

Count resumed episodes when their ending is watched, while excluding seeks and
stalled playback from completion evidence. New shows reach the immersion backend
only after more than 20 minutes of accumulated watch time; their original events
remain durable locally until then. Known shows continue reporting normally.

The hosted interface now counts days with a completed mining episode instead of
player sessions. Multiple completed episodes on one day count as one day.

Windows and Linux downloads are provided here. macOS remains a local installation.
